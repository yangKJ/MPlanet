# 贡献者公约 / Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

我们作为本项目的贡献者和维护者，致力于让参与本项目的体验免于骚扰,不论年龄、体型、残疾、族裔、性别特征、性别认同和表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、个人外貌、种族、宗教或性取向如何。

我们承诺以开放的、友好的、包容的方式参与本项目和我们的社区,无论您是初次提交 Pull Request 的新手,还是经验丰富的资深开发者。

---

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## 我们的标准 / Our Standards

有助于营造积极环境的行为示例:

- 🤝 展现同理心和善意
- 📚 尊重不同的观点、经验和能力
- 🎯 优雅地接受建设性反馈
- 🌱 关注对社区最有利的事情
- 💡 对其他社区成员表示感激

不可接受的行为示例:

| ❌ 不可接受的行为 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| 🚫 使用性别化语言或图像 | 包括不恰当的性暗示或挑逗性言论 |
| 🚫 人身攻击或贬低性评论 | 涉及人身、种族、宗教或其他性质的侮辱 |
| 🚫 公开或私下骚扰 | 任何形式的威胁性、跟踪性行为 |
| 🚫 未经许可发布他人隐私信息 | 例如真实地址、电子邮件地址 |
| 🚫 在专业场合不当行为 | 其他可合理认定为不当或不专业的行为 |

---

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- 🤝 Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- 📚 Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- 🎯 Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- 🌱 Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- 💡 Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

| Unacceptable Behavior | Description |
|---|---|
| 🚫 The use of sexualized language or imagery | and sexual attention or advances of any kind |
| 🚫 Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments | and personal or political attacks |
| 🚫 Public or private harassment | including threatening behavior |
| 🚫 Publishing others' private information | such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission |
| 🚫 Other conduct | that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting |

## 执行责任 / Enforcement Responsibilities

社区维护者有责任澄清和执行我们可接受行为的标准,并将对任何不可接受行为的回应视为适当且公平的回应。社区维护者有权和义务删除、编辑或拒绝与本行为准则不符的评论、提交、代码、wiki 编辑、问题和其他贡献,无论它们是否被视为入侵性、破坏性或不恰当的。

本项目维护团队包括但不限于 `@Condy` 及活跃的 Committers,具体名单请参见仓库 [README.md](./README.md) 的「维护者」一节。

---

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## 适用范围 / Scope

本行为准则适用于:

- 📦 本项目所有代码仓库(`MPlanet` 主仓库及子模块)
- 💬 官方 Issue、Pull Request、Discussion 区
- 🐦 官方社交媒体账号
- 🎤 当您代表项目或社区出席线上/线下活动时

请注意:本项目同时欢迎来自不同背景的贡献者参与,详细参与流程请参阅 [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)。

---

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and it also applies when an individual is representing the project or its community in public spaces. Examples of representing a project or community include using an official project e-mail address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event. Representation of a project may be further defined and clarified by project maintainers.

## 执行 / Enforcement

如果发生任何不可接受的行为,请通过下文「联系方式」中的渠道联系维护团队。所有投诉都会得到及时和公平的审查与调查。

社区维护团队有义务对报告者及被报告者保持保密。在具体执行细节上,我们参照以下分级处理策略:

| 级别 | 处理方式 |
|---|---|
| 🟡 警告(Warning) | 私下书面提醒,要求停止不当行为 |
| 🟠 临时禁言(Temporary Ban) | 在指定时间内禁止项目内一切交互 |
| 🔴 永久封禁(Permanent Ban) | 永久禁止项目内所有公开及私有互动 |

---

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident. The enforcement policies are detailed in the table above.

## 联系方式 / Reporting Guidelines & Contact

如需报告违规行为或有任何疑问,请通过以下任一方式联系维护团队:

- 📧 **邮件**:`conduct@mplanet.dev`(匿名亦可)
- 💬 **GitHub**:`@Condy` 的私信或开启一个标记为 `code-of-conduct` 的私密 Issue
- 🐛 **私密报告**:在 GitHub 中点击 [New Issue](https://github.com/Condy/MPlanet/issues/new) 并勾选「仅维护者可见」

提交报告时,建议包含:事件时间、相关人员、具体描述、是否有目击者。如需更详细的报告模板与流程指引,请参考 [FAQ.md](./FAQ.md) 中的「举报与申诉」一节。

---

For any violations or concerns, please contact the maintainers:

- 📧 **Email**: `conduct@mplanet.dev` (anonymous reports accepted)
- 💬 **GitHub**: DM `@Condy` or open a private Issue labeled `code-of-conduct`
- 🐛 **Private Report**: [New Issue](https://github.com/Condy/MPlanet/issues/new) with maintainer-only visibility

When filing a report, please include: time of incident, people involved, detailed description, and any witnesses. See the FAQ for more guidance.
